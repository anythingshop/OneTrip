Controls

Touch Controls: Left joystick to move, right joystick to rotate and control balance

Keyboard/Mouse: WASD or arrows to move, mouse to rotate and control balance, SPACE to pick up, R to drop

Balance Challenge: Use right joystick (mobile) or mouse movement (PC) to stabilize when tilting

Tips: Get close to bags to pick them up. Ground bags use right hand, car bags use left hand.

Game Over: If you lose your balance while carrying bags, you'll fall and drop everything. You'll recover after 3 seconds.
